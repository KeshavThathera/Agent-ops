# New employee IT onboarding checklist

Owner: Service Desk.

## Day one
- Collect the laptop and sign the asset form.
- Sign in, set a password that meets policy, and enrol multi-factor authentication.
- Connect to CORP-WIFI on campus and test the VPN from outside the office.
- Confirm email, calendar and chat are syncing.

## First week
- Request access to team shares and applications through the access portal.
- Complete the security awareness module.

## Getting help
Raise a support request in AgentOps Desk. Describe the device, what you were doing, and any error message.
