# IT escalation matrix

Owner: Service Desk.

| Area | First line | Escalate to | Target response |
|------|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| VPN, Wi-Fi, network | Service desk | Network Team | 4 business hours |
| Accounts, MFA, passwords | Service desk | Identity Team | 2 business hours |
| Email and collaboration | Service desk | Collaboration Team | 4 business hours |
| Printers and hardware | Service desk | Workplace Services (on-site) | Next business day |
| Security concerns | Service desk | Security Team | Immediate |

## Escalate immediately when
- Many users report the same problem (possible incident).
- A request asks to bypass security controls or delete accounts in bulk.
- Automated verification fails after a remediation.
