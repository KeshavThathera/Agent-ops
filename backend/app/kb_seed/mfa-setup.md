# Multi-factor authentication (MFA) setup and replacement phones

Owner: Identity Team.

## Enrol
1. Install the approved authenticator app on your phone.
2. Sign in to the security portal and choose "Add sign-in method".
3. Scan the QR code and confirm with a one-time code.

## New or lost phone
If you replaced or lost your phone, you cannot approve sign-ins. Contact the service desk; after identity verification an
engineer can issue a temporary access pass so you can enrol the new device.

## Never
Never share one-time codes with anyone, including support staff.
