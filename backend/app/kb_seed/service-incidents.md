# Checking for service incidents

Owner: Service Desk.

Before changing an individual device, check whether the underlying service is degraded. Changing many laptops during a
service outage creates extra work and can hide the real cause.

## Services to check
- VPN gateway, wireless LAN controller, identity provider, mail service, print server.

## If a service is degraded
- Link the ticket to the incident.
- Tell the user about the workaround and expected update time.
- Do not apply device-level fixes until the service recovers.
